Veterinary Knowledge Hub

Clinical Insights & Livestock Care Guide

Practical knowledge and management protocols to enhance animal health, dairy profitability, and companion pet wellness.

Combatting Bovine Mastitis & Udder Edema

Mastitis causes enormous economic losses in dairy herds. Alkaline milk pH disrupts natural immunity and damages secretory alveoli. Combining MASTIMOON Powder (Trisodium citrate & proteolytic enzymes) with VITDHARA-HS (Biotin & Vitamin H) restores physiological pH, heals teat sphincters, and reduces Somatic Cell Count (SCC).

Explore Mastimoon Clinical Details

Optimizing Milk Fat, SNF & Calcium Balance

During peak lactation, high-yielding cows undergo acute mineral drain leading to hypocalcemia (milk fever) and negative energy balance. Supplementing VITDHARA GOLD Liquid ensures rapid bioavailable ionic calcium, while RUMI FAT Powder provides 80% bypass fat to increase butterfat percentage and overall milk output.

View Vitdhara Gold Formulation

Strategic Deworming for Ruminants & Pets

Parasitic burdens cause submandibular bottle jaw edema, anemia, and stunted calf growth. Strategic broad-spectrum dosing with FENDAWORM PLUS or Q-WARM PLUS clears roundworms, tapeworms, and flukes. For puppies and companion pets, ZOHOWORM PET provides safe pediatric worm control from 2 weeks of age.

View Fendaworm Plus Details

Overcoming Sluggish Liver & Anorexia

Post-infection debility and mycotoxins severely suppress ruminal appetite and hepatic metabolism. LIVOMOON-VET delivers standardized herbal extracts of Kalmegh, Bhringraj, and Silymarin to stimulate bile flow, eliminate toxins, and restore hearty feed intake and rumination within days.

View Livomoon-Vet Benefits

Post-Partum Uterine Involution & ROP Care

Retention of Placenta (ROP) and lochial foul discharge invite pyometra and repeat breeding. Administering MOONVET-UT prompts natural uterine contractions to expel retained membranes, cleanses the uterine cavity, and speeds up involution for timely return to fertile estrus.

View Moonvet-UT Profile

Summer Heat Stress & Dehydration Control

Severe summer heat waves lead to panting, electrolyte depletion, acidosis, and sudden milk drop. Providing MOONLYTE Solution with buffered electrolytes and betaine along with BOVIKID PRO amino acids preserves cellular hydration, protects ruminal microflora, and keeps livestock active.

View Moonlyte Solution Details
